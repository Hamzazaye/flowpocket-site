Flowpocket Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not operate any server that receives your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.

What stays on your phone

The server addresses you add, the API keys for those servers (kept in the device secure storage), your settings (theme, language), your Pro status as reported by Google Play, and local backups of workflow versions you saved from the app.

What leaves your phone

The app connects only to the n8n server(s) you add, to read and change your workflows and executions on your behalf. That traffic goes directly between your phone and your server; how it is protected depends on your server (https is recommended). Workflow data you export or share is sent only where you choose to share it, using your phone share sheet. If you contact support by email, the message contains the app version and Android version only.

Notifications

Failure alerts are generated on your phone from data fetched from your own server. No push service run by the developer is used.

Purchases

Optional Flowpocket Pro purchases are made and billed only through Google Play. Flowpocket never sees or stores your payment details; it reads only whether you hold an active Pro purchase, on your device, to unlock features.

Children

The app is not directed to children.

Deleting your data

Remove a server in Settings to delete its API key from the device, or uninstall the app to delete everything.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new text is published at this address.

Contact

Use the developer contact shown on the Flowpocket page in Google Play.
